\# CIT 240 Collaboration Workflow



1\. Repository Initialization: Created and pushed by the owner (`main` branch).

2\. Branching:Each member checked out `main`, pulled updates, and created a unique feature branch (`feature-MEMBER-NAME`).

3\. Contributions:Members edited `index.html` locally using Notepad, committed changes, and pushed their feature branches.

4\. Pull Requests \& Review: Members opened Pull Requests on GitHub, assigned a peer reviewer, and received formal approvals.

5\. Merging \& Syncing: Approved PRs were merged into `main`, and team members pulled the updated `main` branch locally.
